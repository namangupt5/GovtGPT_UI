For family visa renewals in Dubai, the approval authority depends on the emirate where the visa was originally issued and where the renewal is being processed:

Dubai-Specific Renewals:
If your family residence visa was issued in Dubai, the renewal process is handled by the General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs - Dubai (GDRFA Dubai). You must submit your renewal application through the GDRFA Dubai website, smart app, or approved typing centers in Dubai123.

Other Emirates:
For visas issued in other emirates, the Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs & Port Security (ICP) is the relevant authority.
